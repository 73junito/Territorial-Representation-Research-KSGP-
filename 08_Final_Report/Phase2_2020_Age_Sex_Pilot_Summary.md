# Phase 2 — 2020 Age and Sex Descriptive Pilot
Prepared: November 21, 2026

## Study
Six jurisdictions: five permanently inhabited U.S. territories and Washington, D.C. Baseline 2020 Census counts only. Source files archived locally and independently validated.

| U.S. Virgin Islands | 87,146 | 21.84% | 21.28% | 48.59% |
| American Samoa | 49,710 | 40.10% | 6.33% | 50.80% |
| Northern Mariana Islands | 47,329 | 31.63% | 5.90% | 52.59% |

## Interpretation
Population distribution differs across the six jurisdictions, but the counts do not express residents' opinions, potential electoral turnout or policy preferences. Their legal and political status requires separate constitutional and community-led analysis.
