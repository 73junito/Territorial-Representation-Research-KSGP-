**Subject:** Request for Independent Methods Review — Territorial Representation Research

Dear Professor,

I am seeking an independent academic/statistical methods review of a research project titled *Territorial Representation Research*. The project documents 2010–2020 demographic evidence and source-specific socioeconomic measures for five inhabited U.S. territories and Washington, D.C.

The project uses archived U.S. Census Bureau 2020 Island Areas Census profiles and separate ACS/PRCS 2019 one-year and 2016–2020 five-year survey products. My primary goal is to ensure that the data sources, population denominators, statistical uncertainty statements, citations, and limitations are presented accurately before the research is considered for academic or committee circulation.

I would especially value your assessment of the twelve published ACS/PRCS Data Profile percentage margins of error and the two D.C. educational-attainment cases in which a category-level approximation differed from the published estimate. The subsequent verification reproduces those two published margins using direct composite-count margins of error; the underlying covariance structure was not independently reconstructed.

I have prepared a faculty review draft, an evidence and source manifest, a Census methodology and APA reference audit, and a short decision form. The public repository is:

https://github.com/73junito/Territorial-Representation-Research-KSGP-

The project makes no unqualified six-jurisdiction socioeconomic inferential comparison and does not imply any political-party endorsement. The materials are clearly designated as drafts.

Would you be willing to review the methodology and provide written feedback, including any substantive corrections required before further circulation? If this is outside your area or available time, a recommendation for a suitable faculty/statistical reviewer would also be helpful.

Thank you for considering the request.

Respectfully,

Research Project Owner
