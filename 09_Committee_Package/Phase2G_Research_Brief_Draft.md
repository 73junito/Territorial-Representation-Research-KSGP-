# Territorial Representation Research — Committee Brief (Draft)
**Status: FOR REVIEW ONLY — NOT A POLICY ENDORSEMENT**

## Purpose
Provide a factual, transparent source inventory and demographic/socioeconomic context for five inhabited U.S. territories and the federal District of Columbia, while separating the legal status of D.C. from that of territories.

## Established evidence
The six jurisdictions had a combined 2020 Census population of **4,313,440**. Age/sex distributions have been validated against government source data, with existing SPSS evidence retained.

Researchers extracted 32 source-specific socioeconomic observations covering educational attainment, unemployment, median household income and poverty:
- 16 from the 2020 Island Areas Census Demographic Profiles (AS/GU/MP/VI).
- Eight from 2019 ACS/PRCS one-year surveys (PR/DC).
- Eight from 2016–2020 ACS/PRCS five-year surveys (PR/DC).

All original downloads, source positions, government URLs, hashes and reproducible scripts are inventoried.

## Key limits for interpretation
These products differ in sample/census design, income reference periods, adjusted dollars, denominators, population universes and military-housing coverage. **They do not support a single unqualified six-way socioeconomic ranking or significance test.** The findings must be displayed by source family with qualifications, not conflated into policy conclusions.

D.C. is a federal district, not a U.S. territory. Aggregate education, employment, income and poverty measures do not reveal political preferences, citizenship/voting eligibility or views on territorial status.

## Uncertainty
Four median-income confidence intervals directly use published ACS/PRCS 90% margins of error. Twelve derived percentage intervals were calculated using the Census Bureau's methods and independently recomputed, but the **published-profile MOE cross-check is pending**. These must be labeled approximate. An unrelated historic byte-hash difference in the demographic SPSS SAV is documented; its contents still match the validated CSV.

## Committee gate
**GO:** Evidence inventory, reviewed demographic summaries, separate source-specific socioeconomic descriptive findings.
**CONDITIONAL:** Side-by-side presentation with prominent differences in time, source family, population universe and uncertainty.
**NO-GO:** Unqualified all-six socioeconomic rankings, inferential tests or causal/political assertions.

Complete independent published profile-MOE reconciliation, obtain academic methodology review, verify full primary-source citations and record disposition of the SAV preservation exception. Committee endorsement or public release is **not** implied by this draft.
