# Territorial Representation Research — Phase 2H Committee Review Brief
**Status: Draft for discussion only; no committee endorsement or final academic approval**

This research compiles public demographic and socioeconomic data for Puerto Rico, Guam, U.S. Virgin Islands, American Samoa, Northern Mariana Islands and Washington, D.C. (a federal district, not a territory). The six jurisdictions' validated 2020 population totals **4,313,440**.

The project has verified **32 source-specific socioeconomic observations** across education, civilian unemployment, median household income and poverty. The four Island Areas derive from 2020 Census profile files, while D.C. and Puerto Rico derive from 2019 one-year and 2016–2020 five-year ACS/PRCS surveys. Data collection, population universes, military-housing exclusions and inflation conventions differ. These observations **cannot be presented as a statistically standardized six-jurisdiction ranking**.

Directly published median-income margins of error support four survey 90% confidence intervals. Twelve rate intervals were mathematically derived and cross-checked against their own official source count estimates and MOEs; however, independently published profile percentage MOEs could not be downloaded. **The independent MOE reconciliation gate remains blocked (0/12).**

The existing demographic SPSS dataset matches its source CSV by jurisdiction and value. Its byte hash differs from the earlier audit; an exact historical cause has not been verified. This exception is documented without changing the source files.

**Decision:** GO for source-specific descriptive evidence; CONDITIONAL for carefully labeled side-by-side tables; NO-GO for unqualified six-way socioeconomic hypothesis testing, rankings, causal claims or inferred political preferences. External methodology review, complete APA references and published-MOE reconciliation are still needed before public release.
